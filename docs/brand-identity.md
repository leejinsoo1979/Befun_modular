# Befun homepage direction

Updated 2026-10-15. The primary audience is people furnishing a home or personal studio, as selected by the owner.

## Positioning

**내 공간의 수납을, 한 칸씩 구성하다.**

Befun is modular furniture made by combining metal frames and panels. Customers choose dimensions, storage modules, panel colors, and frame finishes in the 3D configurator. The homepage explains these choices before asking visitors to start a design.

## Message hierarchy

1. What it is: metal-frame modular storage for homes and personal studios.
2. What you choose: width, height, depth; open shelves, doors, drawers; panel colors and frame finishes.
3. Where it fits: living-room storage, bookshelves, drawers, lowboards, desks, and wardrobes.
4. How to start: configure in 3D or browse furniture configurations. Review the configuration and KRW-converted amount, then explicitly save a design in the current browser.
5. Questions: contact@befun.co.kr, using the company contact already present in the footer.

## Copy rules

Name the object and action: choose dimensions, combine storage modules, compare colors, rotate the 3D view. General adjectives such as good quality, reasonable prices, and attentive service are not the main reason to choose the product.

The homepage does not present original FLEXCUBE reviews, company counts, ratings, Swiss delivery, manufacturing origin, 200-color availability, free design services, or installation promises as verified Befun achievements. Existing furniture media and the Korean lifestyle campaign remain. AI lifestyle campaign cards retain their editorial labels.

The inspiration is the specificity and sequence of [Builddweller SMS](https://builddweller.com/sms): intended space, product definition, concrete uses, choices, and starting points. Its rental, assembly, materials, pricing policies, and exact copy are not Befun claims.
